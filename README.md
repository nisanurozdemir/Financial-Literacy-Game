# 📊 FinLitSim Pro - Financial Literacy and Portfolio Management Simulation

FinLitSim Pro is an interactive, web-based simulation tool designed to enable users to test asset management strategies in dynamic market conditions by analyzing macroeconomic data. The project combines financial literacy principles with a gamification model to enhance risk management and portfolio optimization awareness.

## 📌 Key Features

- **Flexible Initial Configuration:** Users can freely configure their starting capital (Cash/TRY) before launching the simulation.
- **Diversified Asset Allocation:** To practice portfolio optimization, 4 distinct investment instruments are actively provided:
  - 📈 Stocks (BIST / Global Markets)
  - 🪙 Gold (Safe Haven Asset)
  - 🥈 Silver (Precious Metal with Industrial Value)
  - 💵 Dollar / USD (Foreign Exchange Position)
- **Time-Based Progression:** The simulation operates on a dynamic **12-month (1-year cycle)** period to observe the consequences of short- and medium-term strategies.
- **Inflation Algorithm:** The system applies a continuous monthly inflation penalty on cash reserves, teaching users the principles of the "time value of money" and "preserving purchasing power."
- **🤖 AI-Powered Robo-Advisor:** 
  - **Real-Time Feasibility Analysis:** Evaluates the user's asset allocation each month against hidden macroeconomic scenarios and instantly reports rational vs. irrational decisions.
  - **Next-Month Signal System:** Analyzes market trends to provide users with strategic hints regarding upcoming macroeconomic shifts (interest rate hikes, geopolitical crises, economic growth, etc.).
- **🎯 Post-Analysis (Ideal Strategy View):** Once a month concludes, it reveals the *most ideal financial move* that should have been executed according to that month's market scenario, complete with an educational explanation to build a strong feedback loop.
